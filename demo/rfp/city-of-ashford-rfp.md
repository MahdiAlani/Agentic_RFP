# City of Ashford - Request for Proposal

**RFP No. 2026-114: Enterprise Data Analytics Platform**

Issued by the City of Ashford Department of Information Technology.
Proposals are due by 5:00 PM on November 20, 2026.

## 1. Instructions to Bidders

Respond to every question in Section 3 in the order listed. Keep each response
concise and specific. Marketing material will not be scored.

## 2. Background

The City of Ashford is replacing its on-premises reporting system with a
cloud-hosted analytics platform used by approximately 400 staff across 12
departments.

## 3. Questions

### 3.1 Security and Compliance

3.1.1 Describe how City data is encrypted at rest and in transit, including how
encryption keys are managed.

3.1.2 Does your organization hold a current SOC 2 Type II report? State the end
date of the most recent audit period.

3.1.3 Describe the single sign-on and user provisioning methods supported.

3.1.4 Can all City data, including backups, be stored within Canada?

3.1.5 Describe your process and timeline for notifying the City of a security
incident.

### 3.2 Availability and Support

3.2.1 State your guaranteed uptime and the remedies available if it is not met.

3.2.2 Describe your backup frequency, backup retention and disaster recovery
objectives (RPO and RTO).

3.2.3 Describe your support hours and response times for a critical
(production down) issue.

### 3.3 Implementation

3.3.1 Provide a proposed implementation timeline with major phases.

3.3.2 Describe the training you provide for administrators and end users.

### 3.4 Accessibility and References

3.4.1 Describe your product's conformance with WCAG 2.1 Level AA, and provide a
current VPAT.

3.4.2 Provide three references from municipal government customers of similar
size.

### 3.5 Pricing

3.5.1 Provide annual subscription pricing for 400 users, including any public
sector discount.
