# Corvane Pricing and Licensing

Corvane is licensed as an annual subscription, billed annually in advance.

| Plan       | Price                          | Included                                   |
|------------|--------------------------------|--------------------------------------------|
| Team       | $45 per user per month         | Up to 50 users, 99.9% uptime SLA           |
| Enterprise | $70 per user per month         | Unlimited users, 99.95% uptime SLA, SSO/SCIM, BYOK |

Viewer seats on the Enterprise plan are $12 per user per month.

Public sector organizations receive a 15% discount on list price. Multi-year
agreements of three years lock pricing for the full term.

The standard Enterprise implementation package is a one-time fee of $25,000 and
covers the scope described in the Corvane Implementation Guide.
